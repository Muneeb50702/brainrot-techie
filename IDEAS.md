One site a day → `<slug>.muhammadmuneeb.tech` → one reel.

## The rules
1. **$0 to run.** Everything runs in the browser. No paid APIs, no AI calls, nothing that bills per request.
2. **Your mom gets it in one line.** Simple premise, Gen-Z techie flavor in the jokes, not in the concept.
3. **Real logic under the joke.** Real data or real math, never pure randomness.
4. **A shareable output** (card, score, certificate) with the URL on it.
5. **Own look per site.** New palette + display font every day (override the kit's @theme tokens).
